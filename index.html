SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Smart Attendance Monitoring for Supervisors

Monitor employee attendance, identify workforce issues, and take timely action from one centralized platform.

🎯

Beyond Simple Time Tracking

Traditional attendance tools merely register timestamps. AttendSync is an active supervisor decision-support system that transforms raw clock-in data into actionable operational insights.

🔍

Early Risk Detection

Weighted algorithms analyze chronic tardiness, consecutive absences, and missing punches to score attendance reliability and pinpoint staff requiring immediate supervisor attention.

⚡

Direct Operational Workflow

Seamlessly process leave applications, evaluate punch regularization requests, document supervisor warnings, and record follow-ups with comprehensive database auditing.

MODULE CAPABILITIES

Everything a Supervisor Needs

A comprehensive, rule-based workforce monitoring suite designed specifically for frontline supervisors and department managers.

📅

Attendance Monitoring

Monitor daily employee attendance.

  • Real-time punch logging and shift verification

  • Filter by date and department rosters

Smart Monitoring

Identify low attendance, consecutive absences and unusual patterns.

OPERATIONAL WORKFLOW

How AttendSync Works

A continuous four-stage lifecycle providing closed-loop attendance oversight and accountability.

1

Monitor

Track employee attendance.

Shift punches, daily roster verification, and biometric timestamp logging.
↓
2

Analyze

Identify attendance issues and risks.

Automated rule evaluation flags attendance below 75%, consecutive absences, and high risk scores.
↓
3

Act

Take supervisor action.

Directly approve leaves, review regularization requests, issue formal warnings, or schedule counseling.
↓
4

Record

Maintain an audit trail.

Every decision, timestamp, supervisor note, and status transition is saved to the permanent audit log.

ACADEMIC & TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

Software Development / Database Management

AttendSync is built as a complete frontend prototype incorporating core computer science principles, data structures, and rule-based decision support logic.

Data Structures & Algorithms

Features an interactive Hash Table with Linear Probing collision resolution for O(1) average lookup of employee records and live viva demonstration.

Database & Persistence

Structured schema with 50 realistic employee records, ~1,500 30-day historical punch records, and robust localStorage state management with factory reset.

Supervisor Decision Support

Algorithmic risk engine dynamically computing composite scores (0–100) based on attendance percentages, tardiness frequencies, and consecutive absences.
